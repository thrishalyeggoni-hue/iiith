How to Play — Beginner's Guide
QUANTUM ECHO: THE CITY THAT FORGOT
1

🏃 How to Move & Look

Use W, A, S, D or Arrow Keys on your keyboard, or tap the On-Screen D-Pad buttons at the bottom-left.

💡 Drag anywhere on the screen with your mouse to rotate the camera.

2

⚡ How to Interact

Walk near any glowing terminal (like Terminal Alpha on your LEFT). Press [E] on your keyboard, click the [E] INTERACT button, or directly click the 3D terminal!

3

💡 What is the Echo Lantern?

Your lantern holds a quantum light source (qubit). It can shine in Blue Mode (0), Yellow Mode (1), or a 50/50 Blend (both colors glowing at the same time!). The 3D sphere shows its direction.

4

🛠️ How to Solve Quantum Puzzles

Each machine requires a specific Target State. Click "Set to Blue / Yellow" or apply Quantum Tools (Flip Switch [X], Blender [H], Mirror [Z]) to match the goal, then click "⚡ ACTIVATE CITY MACHINE"!

🎯 For Terminal Alpha: Click "🔵 Set to Blue (0 / Reset)", then click "⚡ ACTIVATE CITY MACHINE"!

5

🦘 How to Jump & Mind the Edge

Press [J] or [Space] on your keyboard, or tap the purple [J] JUMP button to hop onto higher steps, platforms, and bridges.

⚠️ Mind the edges: If you jump outside or fall off the platform into the void, you will die and respawn safely at the checkpoint!
